Stage 04: JoSAA Seat Allotment Rounds (Rounds 1 to 5)

Merit-based seat allocation declared per round based on All India CRL and Category ranks. Candidates must exercise Freeze (accept & end), Float (accept & consider higher choices anywhere), or Slide (accept & upgrade within allotted institute).
